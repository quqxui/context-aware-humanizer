# English Xiaohongshu notes

Use for English-language experience posts, practical guides, comparisons, and product notes for Xiaohongshu. Keep the tone approachable while separating firsthand observations, source claims, and unknowns.

## Worked examples

These fictional examples illustrate editing decisions; they are not model evaluation results.

### Example 1: product information without firsthand use

**Request:** Write a natural product note from the product page, keeping the fact that the writer has not used it.

**Before:** According to the information provided on the product page, this lamp has three brightness levels and a price of ¥129. I have not purchased or tested it.

**After:** The product page lists three brightness levels for ¥129. I haven't bought or tested the lamp.

**Why:** The specifications and price remain attributed to the product page. The writer does not become a customer or a firsthand reviewer.

### Example 2: a limited firsthand observation

**Request:** Make this three-day experience read naturally without turning it into a broader recommendation.

**Before:** I took this 500 ml bottle to work for a period of three days, and the lid did not leak during those three days. That period is too short for me to make a judgment about long-term durability.

**After:** I took this 500 ml bottle to work for three days, and the lid didn't leak during that time. Three days is too short to judge its long-term durability.

**Why:** The capacity, setting, duration, and observation remain. The rewrite does not turn a short trial into a durability verdict or purchase recommendation.

## Choosing the post type

| Type | Approach |
|---|---|
| Personal experience | Use the supplied setting, observations, and limits |
| Practical guide | Organize known steps, conditions, and decision points |
| Comparison | Apply consistent criteria and scope the differences |
| Product note | Attribute supplied specifications and separate them from opinion |

## Writing natural English notes

- Base judgments on supplied observations. Product-page information does not support adding a visit, purchase, trial, or repeat order.
- Keep product names, prices, sizes, dates, conditions, and qualifiers such as “up to” or “during those three days.”
- Use familiar verbs and contractions when they fit the author's style. Do not translate platform catchphrases literally or force slang into every line.
- Preserve the author's spelling variety and natural preferences. A conversational tone does not require exaggerated excitement or invented intimacy with readers.
- Make a title or opening reflect the actual content. Do not add suspense, anxiety, buying pressure, or promises absent from the body.
- Use emojis, hashtags, and lists only when useful or requested. Keep connected ideas in connected paragraphs.
- Keep the conclusion proportional to the evidence. Do not add a preference, audience recommendation, or endorsement to complete a familiar post template.

## Delivery

Return the rewritten note by default. Add a title or bullets only if the request benefits from them; do not expand a short source note into a full review. Leave an already suitable draft unchanged.
